## 2026-11-25 15:05 · Copy buttons per person (replaces Outlook files)

**Updated:** `index.html`

- Removed the Outlook .eml downloads (Mac Outlook opens them as received mail).
- The review screen lists each draft with Copy address, Copy subject and Copy message buttons. The message copies with its formatting and links for pasting into a new email.

## 2026-11-25 14:55 · Email buttons on the Big Sur tab

**Updated:** `index.html`

- The Big Sur tab has Email participants (opens the pre-arrival batch) and Invitation or reminder emails (opens the Big Sur invitation batch).

## 2026-11-25 14:40 · Open drafts in Outlook

**Updated:** `index.html`

- The review screen can download each draft as an Outlook email file (.eml, marked unsent), one at a time or all at once. Opening one in desktop Outlook gives a new, addressed message ready to review and send.
- Single drafts have an Open in Outlook button. The review screen is now titled Review and send.

## 2026-11-25 14:30 · Test copy option for batch emails

**Updated:** `index.html`

- Batch emails can add a test copy addressed to whoever is using the tracker. It appears first in the Google Doc, its subject starts with [TEST], and it is never logged as sent.

## 2026-11-25 14:25 · Document library and Big Sur pre-arrival email

**Updated:** `index.html`

- Email previews have a Documents row: one click adds or removes a document link, and Manage documents adds or removes library entries (stored in `ct_documents`).
- New template "Big Sur pre-arrival: agenda, FAQ, travel", with the agenda and FAQ links preselected. Its batch list ticks everyone marked as coming and lists unconfirmed people unticked.
